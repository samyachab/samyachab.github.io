Project Manager at Sunday, a restaurant payment solution operating in France, the US, and the UK. Led an international support team of 10 across 3 continents, recovered 50k euros in unpaid fees through an automated tracking system, and drastically reduced operational workload through AI-powered automation.

international team management

The team

10 support agents across 6 countries: France, Colombia, Romania, Senegal, Mexico, United States. First point of contact for all restaurant clients via Intercom chat. Managing across time zones, languages, and cultural contexts simultaneously.

Weekly CSS Letter

A weekly newsletter sent to the full support team: performance metrics, key company news, axis for improvement, new help center articles to use as reference. Kept agents aligned and informed across all continents.

Performance reviews and quizzes

Weekly individual performance analysis for each agent. Weekly quizzes sent to challenge and reinforce best practices. Continuous feedback loop to improve response quality and client satisfaction scores.

Slack escalation support

Available on Slack to unblock agents when client situations exceeded standard support scope: product clarifications, financial questions, edge cases. Bridge between the support team and account managers.

process automation and continuous improvement

Debt tracking system (Excel + Salesforce + Metabase)

Built a structured Excel file pulling data from Salesforce and Metabase: client info, total debt, partial debt breakdowns (two distinct debt types), contact details, comments, and weekly contact history. Automated formulas identified the Top 10 clients with highest outstanding debts each week. Every week, those 10 clients were called and their debt evolution updated. Recovered 50k euros in unpaid fees, primarily from payment delays, billing errors, and Stripe mandate expirations.

CSS Letter automation

Automated using DUST and Gemini with Slack and Salesforce integrations. Production time cut from 5 hours to under 1 hour per week.

Ops Letter automation

Monthly performance report for account managers across France, US, and UK. Same AI stack (DUST, Gemini). Production time cut from 3 days to under 3 hours.

Segmented onboarding forms

Replaced the universal onboarding form with multiple targeted links based on the features each new restaurant client had purchased. Reduced friction and account manager workload at each new client signup.

Metabase reporting

Regular data pulls from Metabase to track support team performance, identify bad payers, and feed into weekly reviews. Custom queries built to match operational needs.

training and cross-functional support

Help center articles

One article per week covering product updates, common client issues, and best practices. Used by both support agents and clients directly.

Training decks

PowerPoint training decks for account managers and new hires in France and the US. Designed in Figma, presented live. Built to reduce recurring errors and onboarding time.

Debt recovery calls

Around 5 client calls per week for debt recovery and support escalations when account managers were overloaded. Also prepared legal files: mise en demeure, injonction de paiement, declaration de creance.
