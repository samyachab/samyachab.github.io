July 2024 - Tokyo

On running Japan

Starting from zero contacts in Japan, we mobilized influencers and running communities via Instagram to organize a street run through Shibuya in support of ON’s Track Night Tokyo. Created a full street marketing moment in one of the world’s busiest districts, competed in ON Track Night to attract Japanese runners, and closed the evening with a DJ set in a local bar. Pure community-building from scratch in 5 days.

0 local contacts at start 100+ runners mobilized 50+ at ON Track Night

June 2026 - Los Angeles

Nike & PSG

Following a Nike partnership contract, EightLines collaborated with PSG on a clothing line. Invited to the Maison du PSG in Los Angeles, we organized a 5km run on Hollywood Boulevard bringing 70 people together at 8am, followed by a values talk. Prior to this, we attended Nike HQ in Portland for marketing workshops, sharing our vision of running culture in Europe. Nike Chief Communication Officer Michael Gonda visited our Paris headquarters one month later for a strategy session.

Nike HQ Workshop Portland PSG x Nike x EightLines collab 70 runners at 8am Hollywood

May 2025 - Paris

Latay

A talk with Latay, a podcast on Franco-North-African dual culture, exploring how cultural identity shapes athletic performance and how religion and sport coexist at a high level. Held during Ramadan, the evening combined a recorded talk, a 5km run through Paris, and an iftar dinner prepared for 100 people at the EightLines headquarters. Community, culture, and sport in one evening.

100 people gathered Talk + 5km + Iftar Cultural inclusion at core
